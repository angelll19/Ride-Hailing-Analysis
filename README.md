# OLA Ride Booking Operations Analysis

## Dataset
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hetmengar/ola-and-uber-ride-booking-and-cancellation-data

## Tableau Dashboard
https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/angel.m4598/viz/Book1_17812736216240/Dashboard2?publish=yes


## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes ride-booking operations data to understand how effectively customer demand is converted into completed rides.

The goal is to identify where booking failures occur, quantify their business impact, and uncover operational and behavioural drivers behind unsuccessful bookings.

The analysis combines **Python (data processing & analytics)** and **Tableau (dashboard visualization)** to deliver end-to-end insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Measure booking completion vs failure rates
* Identify where bookings drop in the customer journey
* Quantify revenue impact of unsuccessful bookings
* Analyse root causes of cancellations and incomplete rides
* Understand customer and driver behavioural patterns
* Evaluate whether failed bookings are recovered later
* Recommend operational improvements

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

* Total Bookings: **103,024**
* Customers: **94,544**
* Time Period: **1 month**
* Key Features:

  * Booking status (Completed / Cancelled / Incomplete)
  * Cancellation reasons (driver & customer)
  * Booking value
  * Pickup & drop locations
  * Time-based variables

---

## ⚙️ Tools Used

* Python
* Tableau (Dashboard & Visualization)
* Excel (Initial data checks)

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🚨 Booking Completion Issue

* Only **58.28%** of bookings are completed
* More than **4 in 10 booking attempts fail**

### 💰 Business Impact

* Completed booking value: **₹32.83M**
* Unsuccessful booking value at risk: **₹23.7M**

### 📉 Funnel Drop-Off

* Largest drop occurs during driver confirmation stage
* Significant leakage due to driver-side operational issues

### 🔍 Root Causes

**Driver-related issues:**

* Vehicle breakdowns
* Driver inactivity / delay
* Driver cancellations

**Customer-related issues:**

* Change of plans
* Wrong pickup details
* Passenger issues

### 🔁 Customer Behaviour

* Same-day retry behaviour is extremely rare
* Failed bookings are often not recovered immediately
* Some users repeatedly experience unsuccessful bookings

---

## 📊 Dashboard

The Tableau dashboard includes:

* Booking funnel analysis
* Cancellation breakdown
* Revenue at risk
* Operational KPI tracking

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaway

> Operational and supply-side reliability issues are the primary drivers of booking failure. 

## 📌 Recommendations

* Reduce driver cancellations through incentives & monitoring
* Improve driver availability in high-demand zones
* Address post-acceptance failures (driver inactivity, delays)
* Reduce vehicle breakdown incidents
* Focus on high-failure routes for operational improvement

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

Improving operational reliability has the highest potential to:

* Increase booking completion rate
* Recover lost revenue
* Improve customer experience
* Strengthen platform efficiency
